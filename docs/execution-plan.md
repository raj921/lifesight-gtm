# Execution plan: 30/60/90 days

Every target and cost here is an assumption. Replace them with Lifesight baselines in week 1.

| Days | Milestones | Depends on | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–30: base | Clean and match the TAM. Put scores in Salesforce. Run 3 signals: site activity, measurement hiring, leader change. Weekly Hot list. Inbound alerts. Freeze the holdout. | Salesforce admin, RevOps, vendor access, legal | 95% of accounts match one record. Reps accept 60% of the Hot list. Alerts arrive in 5 min. |
| 31–60: pilot | AI drafts for 2 reps, approved by hand. Readiness check live. Rejections and replies tagged. | 2 reps, Content, approved model provider | 70% of drafts approved. No private activity in an email. |
| 61–90: learn | Retune on outcomes. Add partner signals. Test pages for Hot accounts. Review with Sales leadership. | Outcome data, Sales lead, Marketing | Expand, change or stop, based on results. |

## KPIs at day 90

| Measure | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified meetings | 40 | Main result |
| Meeting rate against holdout | 1.5× | Shows lift, not only activity |
| Inbound to meeting | +25% on baseline | Inbound conversion |
| Opportunities | 20 | Assumes 50% of meetings |
| Pipeline | $1M | Assumes $50k per opportunity |
| Hot inbound alert | Under 5 min | Speed |
| Hot accounts worked on time | 80% | Rep use |
| Research per account | 20 min to 5 min | Sales efficiency |
| Tool cost per qualified meeting | $90–235 | 3 months of tools ÷ 40 |
| Drafts approved | 85% | Quality |

**Holdout limit:** 90 days and 10% of Hot accounts may be too few for a firm answer. Treat it as direction and keep it running.

## Main risks

- **Bad account matches.** Review queue for unclear matches.
- **Reps ignore the list.** Start with 2 reps and change rules weekly with them.
- **Wrong weights.** Show the reason for each score, then retune on outcomes.
- **Wrong email.** Source check, then a person. No automatic sending in 90 days.
